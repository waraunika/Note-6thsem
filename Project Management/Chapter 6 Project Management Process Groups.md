# Process Management Process
## Process
- A process is a sequence of steps taken to achieve a particular goal.
- processes are described in terms of inputs, tools & techniques and outputs
	- inputs: documents, plan, design, artifacts, etc.
	- Tools and techniques: mechanisms applied to inputs
	- Output: documents products service, knowledge, artifacts
## Successful Project Criteria
- Select appropriate process required to meet the project objectives
- Use a method that is well defined and flexible to satisfy requirements
- Establish and maintain appropriate communication and engagement with stakeholders
- Follow requirements to meet stakeholder needs and expectations
- balance the competing constraints of scope, schedule, budget, quality, resources and risk to produce the specified product, service or result.
## Project Management Process
- Concerned with describing and organizing the work of a project
- there are many five processes. they are:
	1. Initiating process: 
		- recognize when project or phase should begin
		- processes performed to define a new project or a phase
		- time spent = 1 - 2 %
	2. planning processes: 
		- design and maintain a plan which leads to successful completion of a project
		- establish the scope of the project and define the course of action required to meet the objectives of the proeject or the phase
		- time spent = 11 - 21 %
	3. executing process:
		- coordinate with people and resources to follow plan
		- complete the work as defined in the project plan
		- 69 - 82 %
	4. monitoring and controlling processes: 
		- monitor and measure progress and take right actions when necessary
		- track, review, and regulate the progress and performance of the project.
		- identify corrective actions if required
		- time spent = 4 - 5 %
	5. closing processes:
		- analyzes completion of the project or phase and its acceptability
		- finalize all activities across all process groups to formally close the project or the phase.
		- time spent = 2 - 3 %

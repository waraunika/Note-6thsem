## W:

| AB \ CD | 00  | 01  | 11  | 10  |
| ------- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 00      | 0   | 0   | 0   | 0   |
| 01      | 0   | 0   | 0   | 0   |
| 11      | *x* | *x* | *x* | *x* |
| 10      | *1* | *1* | *x* | *x* |

W = A (last 2 row)

## X:

| AB \ CD | 00  | 01  | 11  | 10  |
| ------- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 00      | 0   | 0   | 0   | 0   |
| 01      | *1* | *1* | *1* | *1* |
| 11      | *x* | *x* | *x* | *x* |
| 10      | *1* | *1* | *x* | *x* |
X = A + B (last 2 row = A, 2nd row = A'B)

## Y:

| AB \ CD | 00  | 01  | 11  | 10  |
| ------- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 00      | 0   | 0   | *1* | *1* |
| 01      | *1* | *1* | 0   | 0   |
| 11      | *x* | *x* | x   | x   |
| 10      | 0   | 0   | *x*   | *x*   |
Y = BC' + B'C = B XOR C (2 squares, from 1st 2 column, and last 2 column)

## Z:

| AB \ CD | 00  | 01  | 11  | 10  |
| ------- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 00      | 0   | *1* | 0   | *1* |
| 01      | 0   | *1* | 0   | *1* |
| 11      | x   | *x* | x   | *x*   |
| 10      | 0   | *1* | x   | *x*   |
Y = CD' + C'D = CD' + C'D = C XOR D (last column = CD', 2nd column: CD')
